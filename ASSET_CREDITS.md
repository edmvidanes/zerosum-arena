# Asset credits

ZeroSum Arena uses no third-party sprite packs and no copyrighted characters.

- Fighters, auras, energy effects, and the logo are original vector art drawn
  in code (SVG/CSS) inside this repository.
- Sound effects and music are synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio API.
- The Dragon Ball inspiration is limited to genre tropes (charging auras,
  energy beams, tournament ladders); no Dragon Ball assets, names, or artwork
  are used.
